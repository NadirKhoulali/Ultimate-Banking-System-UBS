Ultimate Banking System 2.0

A physical banking, retail, security, smartphone, and multiplayer heist platform for NeoForge 1.21.1.

Banking and Commerce

  • Multi-account banking, ATMs, tellers, payment terminals, cards, wallets, cash, cheques, loans, and central-bank policy
  • Player-owned banks with staffing, premises, vaults, alarms, access logs, lending, and compliance
  • Player-owned shops with claims, stockrooms, displays, cashiers, deliveries, webshop checkout, and opening hours

Smartphone

  • Primary and per-account banking apps with separate PIN sessions
  • Realtime/offline messenger, contacts, typing status, pay requests, and money gifts
  • Lock screen, password, notifications, notes, calculator, paint, themes, and optional mod bridges

Vaults and Security

  • Safety-deposit rows, private viewing rooms, physical trays, size pricing, rent, and protected bank premises
  • Standing/compact safes, cash stacks, gold and silver bars, metal pallets, and market valuation
  • RFID readers/cards, access levels, programmable signal targets, per-player passage, and audit state

Bank Heists

  • Multiplayer crews, planning, readiness, masks, lockpicks, OVE9000 saws, drills, and computer hacking
  • Physical deposit-box, safe, and pallet loot with duffel bags
  • Bank alarms, tactical HUD, extraction zones, cooldowns, abandonment, and recovery

For Servers and Developers

  • Unified tactical claim tools and configurable claim visibility
  • Bounded transaction history to reduce save-time data growth
  • Java API 2.0 for finance, notifications, prices, banks, shops, and heists
  • No embedded HTTP server or browser-based administration panel
